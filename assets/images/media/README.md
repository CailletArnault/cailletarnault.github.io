# Media Logos

Logo sources and coverage links are recorded in `_data/media_coverage.yml`.
Files are stored locally to avoid hotlinking. Le Figaro, Les Echos, and Clubic
wordmarks were extracted from their publishers' inline SVGs. White wordmarks
are adapted to dark ink for the page's white background, with their original
geometry preserved. The TF1, France 2, M6, Le Parisien, and Le JDD artworks are
public copies of the broadcasters' and publishers' official logos.
Paperjam's SVG canvas is fitted to the artwork to remove built-in whitespace.
